## Vision

### 10x Check
Moolah should not become a slightly better collection of fantasy leagues. It should become a city-anchored sports club for globally dispersed young adults, with leagues as the wedge, content as the public surface, identity as the retention engine, and local perks/events as the monetization bridge.

The World Cup is not just a campaign. It is a temporary global attention engine that feeds people into a long-term club. Colombo is not just a city with many members. It is the first place where Moolah can become undeniable in real life. The member portal is not just a dashboard. It is the mythology machine that makes titles, rivalries, and history feel permanent.

The 10x version is: people join for one competition, stay for a world, and start to feel that Moolah is part of how they live sports, friendships, and city life.

### Platonic Ideal
A year from now, Moolah feels like a living sports civilization.

A prospect discovers it through a reel, a rivalry clip, a World Cup campaign page, or a friend. They understand the immediate offer quickly: a live competition with real stakes and real people. They pay, enter, and are not dropped into chaos. They are welcomed into a world with clear status, city identity, and next steps.

Inside, the club feels deep. Champions are memorialized. Rivalries have receipts. City tags mean something. The portal feels like entering a hall of fame, not opening a settings panel. Discord stays alive through daily micro-rituals and biweekly editorial beats. Colombo has visible gravity, perks, meetups, inside jokes, and partner value. Sponsors do not buy posts. They buy measurable behavior inside a trusted local club.

This is the ideal: a premium sports-culture brand where belonging is the soul, glory is the hook, and local density turns community into a business.

## Accepted Scope (added to this plan)
- Treat the 2026 FIFA World Cup as a paid acquisition and conversion system into long-term Moolah membership behavior.
- Make Colombo the flagship city and use Oslo or another city as a seeded outpost, not an equal launch theater.
- Build Moolah around a 3-layer product structure: Community, Competition Member, Club Member.
- Build a public site and a separate member portal rather than one blended website.
- Make the member portal world-first and identity-first, with champions, history, rivalry, titles, and mythology as the center.
- Create a cross-sport status and identity layer so Moolah compounds across seasons and sports.
- Create a layered access model: open outer funnel, curated inner club.
- Define explicit member archetypes so competitors, club/social members, and city/perks members all belong legitimately.
- Define a shared membership spine built on identity plus recurring ritual.
- Run a biweekly major editorial ritual inside Discord, supported by daily micro-rituals like Wordle and ambient participation.
- Treat content as core product surface, not just marketing support.
- Package sponsor value city-first and measure it through real local behavior.
- Add a founder scorecard to track city density, active core, conversion, content, and sponsor proof.

## Core Strategic Calls

### What Moolah is
A sports-culture club with fantasy sports as the first engine.

### What Moolah is not
Not a fantasy platform. Not a discount club. Not a content page. Not a Discord with side projects attached.

### Brand doctrine
- Public truth: friendship, distance, belonging, human connection.
- Conversion hook: glory, rivalry, competition, something to earn.
- Member world: mythology, status, identity, history.

### Company proof sequence
Run World Cup acquisition and city density in parallel, but when there is a tradeoff, choose the move that makes Colombo more real.

### Public funnel
Instagram -> public landing page -> clear World Cup offer -> payment -> guided Discord entry -> structured onboarding -> long-term path into leagues, city identity, and club membership.

### Website split
- Public site: acquisition, story, campaigns, current leagues, city proof.
- Member portal: GM profiles, rivalry, titles, records, history, lore, future perks/status.

### Density model
- Colombo Local Core
- Colombo Orbit
- Moolah Active Core

### Near-term sport portfolio
NBA, WNBA, F1, World Cup, Premier League. Then stop. Build depth.

## Founder Scorecard Draft
The founding team should review these weekly:
- Colombo Local Core count
- Colombo Orbit count
- Moolah Active Core count
- New paid competition entries
- World Cup -> next-league conversion rate
- Member-generated lore moments captured
- Content reach that converts into site visits or paid entries
- Sponsor/perk pilot redemptions and repeat usage
- Referral-driven member acquisition
- Biweekly ritual publish rate and engagement

## Risks
- Too many sports too soon turns the club into a bundle instead of a world.
- Sponsor thinking too early makes the product smell like inventory.
- A weak member portal reduces mythology to reporting.
- An open top-of-funnel without a curated core dilutes the club.
- A weekly editorial cadence burns out the founding group.

## What this plan changes
The original design positioned Moolah as a league wedge that could later become a club membership. This CEO review expands that into a sharper company thesis:

Moolah should be built as a city-first sports club with league competition as the entry wedge, mythology as the retention engine, and local partner economics as the monetization layer.

### Discord engagement ingestion after core funnel stabilizes

**What:** Ingest Discord engagement signals, including XP or equivalent bot-driven activity, into the owned member system and founder scorecard after the Tranche 1 funnel, payment flow, member core, and Discord onboarding are stable.

**Why:** Community status is not only competition status. Moolah also needs a way to recognize and later reward the members who power the club through chat activity, rituals, and ambient participation.

**Pros:** Lets the scorecard reflect real club vitality, enables future rewards for high-engagement members, and creates a bridge between Discord-native stickiness and the owned identity layer.

**Cons:** Adds integration complexity and should not block the first launch tranche.

**Context:** This was explicitly discussed during the engineering review after the founder described Discord XP and the idea of rewarding the highest-engagement member with a real-world prize. It depends on the Tranche 1 member core being stable first.

**Depends on / blocked by:** Tranche 1 member system, recoverable onboarding flow, and snapshot-based founder scorecard shipping first.
